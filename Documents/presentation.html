Introducing Health AI

Ask Healthcare Questions
Clear, conversational answers for symptoms, preventive advice, and wellness inquiries.

Locate verified facilities, operational hours, direct phone numbers, and emergency hotlines.

Current Limitations

What Is Not Available Yet

Future Scope

Health AI is currently focused on information and discovery. The following capabilities are planned for future phases:

1. Hospital Registration
Direct patient profile creation with partner hospitals.
2. Queue / Ticket Booking
Getting queue tickets before visiting facilities.
3. Doctor Search
Finding doctors based on specialty and schedule.
4. Appointment Booking
Scheduling clinical outpatient appointments.
5. Patient History
Accessing past interaction and visit history.
6. End-to-End Service
Prescription fulfillment, insurance, and billing.
Key Message We are starting with information assistance and gradually expanding toward healthcare services.
